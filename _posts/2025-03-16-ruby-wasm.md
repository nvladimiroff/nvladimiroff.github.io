title: Replacing your frontend with Ruby (with WASM!)
---

Wanna ditch JavaScript and replace your frontend with Ruby? Me too! Unfortunately, Ruby isn't there yet. But if you're willing to trek through some untrodden paths, I'll show you how to create the most basic of single page applications:

```ruby
app = JS.global[:document].getElementById("app")
app[:innerHTML] = '<h1>Hello world!</h1>'
```

High-tech!
